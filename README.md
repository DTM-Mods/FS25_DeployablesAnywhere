### Free placement (Ctrl+Shift+P)

Off by default, so nothing changes by accident. In construction mode, pick an item and press **Ctrl+Shift+P**; the F1 help shows whether it is on. While it is on, these checks are skipped:

- land you do not own
- overlap with other objects
- restricted, shop and spawn areas
- water
- the item's own "cannot be placed here" check
- blocked terrain leveling (the item is placed without flattening the ground)

The price at the cursor names the check that is skipped, for example *"(free placement: Land is not owned by you)"*.

Still checked: money, building slots, your farm's build permission, and items that cannot be bought.

### Precise positioning (buildings and decorations)

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+Shift+G | Place without flattening the ground (no leveling cost). Items on slopes keep the natural terrain. |
| Ctrl+Shift+Arrow keys | Nudge the item 10 cm away from/toward the camera or left/right. The first nudge locks the item in place, so the mouse no longer moves it. |
| Ctrl+Shift+PageUp / PageDown | Raise or lower 5 cm, without limit. Raised or lowered items are placed without flattening. |
| Ctrl+Shift+Comma / Period | Rotate 1°. |
| Ctrl+Shift+Backspace | Reset nudge, height and rotation. |

Hold a key to repeat. Everything resets when you pick another item or leave construction mode. The keys can be changed in **Settings > Controls**.

### Selling

Your farm's buildings, decorations and fences can always be sold in construction mode (demolition mode, **B**), also on land you do not own. No key needed. Items of other farms and of the map cannot be sold. Removed fence segments and panels are refunded at what they cost to build; the base game pays nothing back for fences.
